# §492. One mask copied, and the pick's sample

*2026-11-01*

Two asks from discussion 59's list (2026-11-01).

**One mask, moved about.** The mask list had no menu; it gains a
right-click one (Ctrl+click on a Mac), built as the collections menu
is. Duplicate mask puts a copy below its mask under a new id and the
name a new mask would get, every shape, slider, invert, and switch the
same, its learned rasters carried so the model does not run again,
one history step. Copy mask holds one mask on a clipboard of its own,
apart from the settings' Ctrl+C, so neither replaces the other. Paste
mask joins it to the open frame and the selection through the settings
paste's own path, one "Paste from" step a frame, the AI shapes made
again on each frame, a frame that already holds that exact mask left
alone as Add to masks leaves it, and the pasted mask keeping its name.
A join now stops at the sixteen masks a picture can draw, on the sync
sheet as well, where Add to masks could pass it before; `MAX_MASKS`
in the edit is the one number render's limit is defined from.

**The pick's sample.** The Color shape's pick averaged a 5x5 square;
a stray pixel could win. A Sample control beside Pick offers 5, 11,
21, or 41, kept in the settings, not the edit, and a light-and-dark
square at the pointer shows the area while picking. The sizes are the
picture's own pixels, since the viewport develops at full size, so 5
is exactly what it was and the square follows the zoom; at fit on a
6000-pixel frame, 41 is about seven screen pixels. The other droppers
keep their 5x5.
