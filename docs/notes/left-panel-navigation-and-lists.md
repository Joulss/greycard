# The left panel's navigator size and lists

*2026-11-12*

The Loupe's Navigator preview stays centered and at most 260 px wide
when the left panel grows. Its slot's height follows that limit rather
than the panel's full width.

**Denser lists.** Folders loses its inner frame and padding; its
disclosure icon uses the panel icon size. Presets, Snapshots, and History
have shorter rows and tighter spacing, without their former inner
frames. Snapshot actions and the rename field fit the shorter rows;
the preset removal question and the actions below its list have small
gaps of their own.

The Library and Loupe switch, scrolling lists, and footer use narrower
gutters. The scroll area follows the visible panel width instead of
acquiring horizontal scrolling from its contents.
