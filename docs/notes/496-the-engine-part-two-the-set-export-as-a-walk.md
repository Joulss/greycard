# §496. The engine, part two: the set export as a walk

*2026-11-01*

The second of §478's three landings: the export of a frame that is
not the open file, which is every frame of a headless export, is a
walk of its stage list under the export's purpose, on the CPU and
from nothing kept, finished by the one CPU finish. The bar grew from
28 to 40 exports to cover what this path runs: the learned tier with
a Subject mask, X-Trans with the learned tier, a Fill with no LaMa
beside a Heal and a Subject mask with its own Detail, a turned
straightened crop, a JPEG picture turned with a crop, and a frame
with no sidecar. All 40 are byte-equal to master's, the models run
for real on the CPU, exit codes and every warning line the same.

**The models, for real.** The walk's `Models` is the set's own: the
learned denoiser through the disk cache by a `network` the window's
develop now shares (cache read, run, cache write, the same key, the
same Ran or Cached), the fills through the engine's fill with its
report and unmade ids, the learned masks made then from the patched
picture with the retouch that made it. What the export went without
is said in the words the develop and the finish used, in the same
order, learned, gave-way, fills, look, masks, so a log reads as it
did; the export's info line carries the denoiser's provider and the
fill counts again, through the develop's own helpers, after the first
build lost them. A stand-in for a missing model is decided before the
walk, so a frame without the model walks the list without the learned
tier from the start rather than preparing twice. A fill model asked
whether it could fill more takes the models mutably, as the fill
itself does.

**What is kept.** Nothing for a later walk but the patched picture,
and that only where a learned mask is made beside a Fill patch, since
only Fill patches reach the masks; the sharpen's mask is dropped
before the finish. The peak over the bar is below master's on
thirty-seven frames of forty and about equal on the rest.

**Still the old way.** The open file's export reads the last develop,
its base, and its learned pair; it moves with the main develop in the
last landing, whose reviewer's watch list is: the unmade fill ids and
the stand-in belong in the kept point and the meta, the Learned stage
should clear the gave-way itself, a missing model must be known before
the kept points are cleared, and the export's list will not
prefix-match the viewport's at the masks tap and the stripped keeps,
so lists compare modulo purpose-only steps or the retirement of the
last develop has nothing to resume from.
