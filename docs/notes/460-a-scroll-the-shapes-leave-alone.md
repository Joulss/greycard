# §460. A scroll the shapes leave alone

*2026-10-31.* The grid lays its rows out by the pictures' shapes
(0.9.0), and when shapes come in after the layout it lays out again
and moves the scroll so what was on screen stays, the selected
frame brought back whole when it was on screen at all. Over a large
root the pictures keep coming as the rows come on screen, and a
notch of the wheel that cut the selected row at the top was undone
by the next picture: the grid jittered and never went down. Now the
re-flow brings the selected frame back whole only when it was whole
before the rows moved; one the wheel has cut at an edge is held
where the wheel put it, as any other row is.
