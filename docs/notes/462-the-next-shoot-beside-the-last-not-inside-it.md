# §462. The next shoot beside the last, not inside it

*2026-10-31.* Back up's default destination (§219) falls, with no
pairing of the folder's own or of one above it, to the most recent
backup from the same root: the folder as a sibling of that one,
its destination's parent with the folder's name. That takes the two
for siblings. A day folder backed up under its shoot as
`<year>/<shoot>/<day>`, then the next shoot from the root, got
`<year>/<shoot>/<next shoot>`: the day's parent is the first shoot,
not the year. Now the pairing is read back up a level at a time,
while the destination keeps the source's name, to the folder the
two sources share, and the folder's path under that folder goes
under the destination there: the next shoot lands at
`<year>/<next shoot>`, a day of it at `<year>/<next shoot>/<day>`.
Where the names part ways above the pairing, a shoot renamed on
the archive, the folder is a sibling of the renamed one as before.
